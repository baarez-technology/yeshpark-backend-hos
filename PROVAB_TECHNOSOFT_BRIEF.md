**Subject: AI-Based Hospitality Platform - CRS Connectivity & OTA Integration**

**Platform Overview:**

We are an AI-powered hospitality management platform offering comprehensive CRS connectivity and seamless OTA integrations. Our solution enables real-time booking synchronization, automated channel management, intelligent revenue optimization, and bidirectional data flow across multiple distribution channels including Booking.com, Expedia, and other major OTAs. The platform features webhook-based event processing, real-time availability updates, rate synchronization, and automated inventory management. We provide a unified dashboard for monitoring channel performance, booking analytics, and sync status across all connected systems. Our architecture supports RESTful APIs, webhook receivers, and Server-Sent Events for live updates. We seek partnership for enhanced CRS connectivity and expanded OTA integration capabilities.
